> All the lessons below intertwine and interconnect with each other, leading to the same goal: ***how to live a better life and be a better person***.

**Don’t be a crab.** If you’re unaware of the infamous crab mentality, watch [this](https://youtu.be/BdqwSDoMOdM?feature=shared). tl;dr: It’s the mindset where if you don’t succeed, you try to bring others down. I was not a crab in the exact sense in that I tried to bring others down. But as much as it’s hard to admit, I was rarely happy for others’ success. Always thinking, why not me? And, this not only reflects in your relationships with others but also your own life. This exact mindset and some other reasons are the answers to the question of why not me. I needed to realize that others’ success is a good thing. It’s a good thing collectively for society, the nation, and humanity as a whole. And that my success depends solely upon my actions and a little luck. It was futile not to cheer others’ success. As long as I did the right thing for myself, there was every reason to celebrate others’ hard work, and later we’d celebrate mine. But if you were a pessimistic, lazy, and grumpy person like me, then it’d be hard for you to do this, and so the only way to change it is (as given in the next point).  


**Get out of your head.** I was mostly lazy, pessimistic, grumpy, and inconsistent because I was always stuck in my head: overthinking, over-planning, and over-analysing. These prevented me from taking action and being disciplined, as I always got in my way, stopping myself way before any habit could be made or result could be seen. Self-doubt is the dream killer. Being stuck in your head is a big source of your anxiety. Anxiety happens when you know what you need to do, but you’re not doing it because you’re scared, doubtful, or lazy. Action kills anxiety. Stop thinking so much. It’s futile. Most rewards, lessons, and insights are found in action and when you’re in the arena. When you’re on the way, not idle. Of course, you don’t need to take off at this moment itself.

Take some time out to **find your North Star**: 3–4 things that are the most important things you need to do, have, or be in your life right now, and get them. Don’t wait for the entire map to be clear in front of you before you get started. That will never happen. But once you know a destination/direction that you’d like to go in, get started now. Take the first step. You already kill half of your anxiety and render many of the next steps just with your first step. Actions lead to the greatest insights, and the way presents itself with each next step. Keep taking one step after the other with a moment of reflection. The first step is also the hardest step: it has a very high threshold of barriers that you need to cross. And, as *Abel Morales* says in the movie ‘A Most Violent Year’: “when it’s scary to jump (Ian), that’s exactly when you jump. Otherwise you end up staying in the same place your whole life. And that I can’tdo”. But the first step is also the most fruitful. So, get started now. 10% thinking and reflecting, 90% action and execution. You can time-block the former for each day and the rest of the day, don’t be slacking, keep taking action. Doing. And, *don’t ever let petty or small things occupy your mind*. They will come and go. Only let your mind be occupied by your 3–4 most important things at your current point in life.

**Don’t compare yourself or your life to others**. This is again futile. I’ve spent a lot of my time questioning God, the universe, or myself as to why the cards that I was dealt in life, or why my circumstances were so bad in life, compared to others I knew: my friends, peers, or acquaintances. And, I’ve never gotten back an answer for it. And, the more time I spend pondering upon this question, the further it sets me back. So, it’s a negative prophecy. A dumb move. And as the Chinese proverb says: “*the rice grains are cooked, what’s done cannot be undone”*. You can’t change the past; you can only change what’s ahead. If you no longer want to be stuck in the unhappy place or state that you are in, start acting and stop whining about your situation, as well as comparing yourself to others. A big reason to stop comparing is that the greatest thing about each one of us in this world is our uniqueness. That is our moat, strength, and our guiding light in life. Each of us has a unique life to live. Embrace it, celebrate it. Create and chase your own unique goals, principles, and desires.

**Follow your heart and tap into your uniqueness**. To compare is to copy. And if you’re copying someone, you can never find true success, as you’ll never think for yourself. As Naval Ravikant says, “no one can compete with you on being you”. So, tap into it and you will find and fulfill your life’s purpose as well as find success. Follow your heart to find your North Star. Don’t try to blend into society’s expectations, validations, or status if it doesn’t align with your heart. It’s the path to misery. Being honest with yourself and following your heart at all times will always lead you to becoming happier, more peaceful, free, and excited for life. If for any reason, you can’t follow or fulfill your heart’s desires right now, do everything in your power to overcome the obstacles in front of you so that eventually you can live according to your heart’s desires. If you follow your heart, you’ll do what you love. And, if you do what you love, you’ll reach great heights almost effortlessly, for “*the man who loves walking will walk further than the man who loves the destination”*. Tap into your heart’s desires and your unique interests & strengths for success and happiness. And,

**Give 101% to everything you do in life**: **work, fun, relationships, and health**. When you’re working, work the hardest. When you’re having fun, have the most fun. Give 101% to all the relationships that you value in life, even if it isn’t reciprocated. At the end of the relationship (be it with friends, family, or love) didn’t work out as you desired, you’ll have no regret because you always gave your best. You did what was in your hands up to its highest potential. But you can’t control others. Don’t leave a chance for regret. And all regrets are almost always: *I wish I had done this, or I wish I had done more*. Give your 101% to every moment and everything, and let regret be non-existent in your life. Of course, easier said than done. But let’s try this so that we die peacefully. On a similar note…

**Don’t wait for yourself and your life to be perfect before you start to have fun, be happy, or love and express yourself**. My father lived like this. He worked and worked postponing happiness, family, travelling, and his desires for later when things are stable and easier, but that later never came. I, too, have lived similarly all this while, and I want to change that. Of course, if you’re going through hell in life, you should do everything in your power to keep going and get out of that situation. But at the same time, this is your only life. And life is uncertain for you as well as for those in your life that you love and value. Give your everything every day and every moment to get to a better place in life, but don’t wait to love and accept yourself after you’ve achieved something. Firstly, you don’tknow when that will happen for sure. And secondly, most of us do not have well-described finish lines as a result of which the finish line keeps extending itself, and we never really feel like we’ve made it. When in fact, you could be living a dream of your past self right now, which you may not even realise. So, don’t wait until you have some accolades¹, are good-looking, wealthy, or great before you love yourself, express yourself, and your feelings to someone you like or with your friends on social media. Be happy now as you are in your imperfect life and self, and be happier tomorrow when you’re even better. Even if what your experiences are right now are common for others, celebrate it, share it, and embrace it, for it is your first time, and your life is all about your unique journey. Don’tplay it down like you’ve done all this while. Keep supporting and loving yourself as you are, and you will reach great heights with absolutely unique experiences. Be happy now to be happier later.

**You are as great and capable of doing great things as the greatest person to ever walk this planet**. As Steve Jobs once said, *“Life can be so much broader, once you discover one simple fact, and that is that everything around you that you call ‘life’ was made up by people who were no smarter than you”*. There’s no difference between you and the greatest person to ever live on this planet as long as you have a fully functioning mind and body². Many people have achieved greatness and done gigantic things even without a fully functioning mind or body. Everything around you — the internet, appliances, your bed, democracy, weapons, movies, laws, morals, etc was built by people just like you, and you can do it too. Thinking small and thinking of yourself as incapable of greatness and doing great things is a self-fulfilling prophecy that prevents you from taking action, continuing to be lazy, and being influenced by other people’s actions and the world around you when, in fact, you could be the one shaping it (if that interests you). Don’t ever let a single moment of self-doubt creep in on you. You can do anything you set your mind to, with the 3 P’s: Patience, Persistence, and Practice. The world is yours. Seize your life. You are capable of all things great.

And, soon enough, you will be them. No more needing to assume. Of course, consistent action and measurable/visible growth reduce this gap and reinforce/change your identity slowly and steadily to that which you desire. But why come from a place of lack or gap at all? Why let there be friction? Instead, assume you already are that successful person and live like one. Lie until you no longer have to.
