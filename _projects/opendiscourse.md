---
title: OpenDiscourse
year: 2026
---

A free Chrome and Firefox extension that adds an anonymous comment layer to every webpage. No sign-up — you get a persistent identity, threaded replies, quoted text selections, and @mentions.

[GitHub](https://github.com/satyamsundaram/opendiscourse) · [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/opendiscourse/ephbdbchjgmneffigpmpfifkhpaeolee) · [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/opendiscourse/)
