As I turn 24 today, I wanted to reflect on the life that I’ve lived so far to understand my mistakes, learnings and desires. So, that I can apply better methods to the life that I’m yet to live. And, as I think about it, the most important thing I would like for me or anybody else like me to remember and follow is to embrace your life truly. Let me explain what I mean by that and how that came to be my most important lesson.

Over the last few years, my life has taken a massive downturn with a few upward spikes in between. I was not responsible for it but it was the cause of the events in my surroundings and of course my fate. At first, I could not come to terms with it. But slowly over time, I have. I could not come to terms with it because I could not comprehend why “I” had to suffer and not anybody else that I knew. But, I did not find any answer to that — except that it was not in my control, not my doing and it was just pure luck. And, the only thing that’s in my control is how I react to the events that happen to me. By focusing on what I control and by understanding that my current situation was not my doing, I felt slightly better. But, there’s more.

The more I wasted time trying to understand why this happened to me and sulking in my sadness, the more chances I missed to fix it. I needed to understand that this is not the end. That I have the power to change it and make it so much better than it ever was, is or I could ever imagine. Growth mindset as I mentioned [here](/2024/07/05/practices-to-transform-life.html). So, now I had accepted my fate and decided to do all that was in my power to change it for the better. But, there was one more hurdle I needed to cross to get there—the hurdle of comparison and envy.

As I mentioned before, I thought about why it happened to me and not to others. I was comparing my present with the present of the people I know. And, they were already living my dream life in some ways. What was special for me, was normal for them. But, what helped me overcome this hurdle was understanding that I didn’t envy all the people in this world. Even the people I did envy, I didn’t want to swap my entire life with them. I envied only certain parts of their lives. And, there are so many people in this world for whom my current life might be their dream. There are no limits to this.

Even if somebody I knew already had what I desired and my dreams were their normal life, this shouldn’t stop me from celebrating when I do achieve any of my dreams. But I have stopped myself from celebrating on several such occasions, only because it was not so special for the people I knew. This was a really stupid and hurtful move on my part. As long as I kept doing this, I would never be happy in life and I needed to stop it. I needed to live my life from my perspective, and not from anybody else’s. I needed to write the story of my life without diluting it or diminishing it. So, I did. Now, I had accepted my fate and that it was not my fault. I was ready to change it and make it as good as it can be by giving my best. I was going to embrace my life in all its wreckage and all its glory. Because this is my one and only life. And, I must love it, stand for it, fight for it, and appreciate it because otherwise what’s the point? Who will, if not me?

And, so now I walk the path of my life that’s been laid out before me. When I look at others living their best lives, I don’t feel envy. I find it inspiring because every day I too, am walking and working towards my best life, as well as appreciating myself today as I am. I know exactly what I want and am going after it. I am super focused but patient as well. I live my life and celebrate my wins only by comparing my today with my past and how far I’ve come. The only comparison is with who I was. There is no concern for what anybody else thinks of me because honestly, nobody thinks about nobody but themselves. I believe in myself. I do what I want and what’s best for me. And, I finally feel happy, alive and present today as I am and aspire to feel the same tomorrow as whatever I will be. I hope you do too.
