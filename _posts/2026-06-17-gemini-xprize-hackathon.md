I came across this [hackathon](https://xprize.devpost.com) a few days ago while I was lurking on Devpost for a hackathon to participate in. Above all, I wanted something to actually complete and make a real submission (even if I don't end up winning). And this somehow got me excited as it required to create a business with real users and revenue which I would love to try. Although, it requires a major part of the business to be operated by AI agents (at least many key operations or functionalities).

At first, I remembered my agent which I had created at work whose job was to acknowledge queries posted in a slack channel and then go on to find answers for that query across our entire infrastructure for which it has access via tokens or MCP servers. The core part of it aka searching across multiple services, components and tools was done by a claude subprocess all on its own and replying to the user with the response.
