I'm 25 years and 8 months old today. And, I feel like I'm at a pivotal moment in my life. That things are going to drastically change from here. I'm really close to finally getting a job at the company that I wanted to get into, since the last 2 years or so, ever since I heard about it. I could not make it in, around 1.5 years ago when I'd interviewed then. But this time I have. And even my pay has substantially increased, another thing which I very much desired: a fair value for the work I put in. But more importantly, I wanted to work with smart people, and solve real, interesting challenges that keep me up at night. Because what else is the point of working if you're not stimulated and challenged beyond your comfort zone. And in turn grow into a better person. That's the part I'm most excited and also nervous about: the challenges that lie ahead of me. But, I'm also ready to face them head on and win, even if I have to fail a few too many times.

Well this turned into a pep talk. Anyway, I had dreamed and desired of this for quite some time and now it's finally so close to happening. And, I'll give my best to it. Of course, this is just the start of the upwards trajectory but I just wanted to take a moment to acknowledge and reflect on this achievement and on what lies ahead of me.
