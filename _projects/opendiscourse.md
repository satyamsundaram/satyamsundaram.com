I often read technical or opinionated blogs and wanted to know what others thought about it. Or share my own thoughts in some conversation. But for the current state of the internet, many of these websites did not have any comment section integrated and all conversation around it happens on Reddit, X or Hacker news by linking the URL of the blogpost. And, while this is good, I wanted something self-contained within the blog page itself.
